Welcome to my personal page!

My name is Daniela and I am a Researcher in the Language and Dialogue Technologies ([LanD](https://land.fbk.eu/)) group at Fondazione Bruno Kessler ([FBK](https://www.fbk.eu/en/)) in Trento, Italy. My work in Information Engineering and Computer Science is focused on Natural Language Processing and Generation. I hold a bachelor and a master's degree in Digital Humanities from the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/en/), where I specialised in Language Technologies. My research centers on persona-based dialogue generation, exploring how dialogue agents can adapt to different speaker profiles to enhance conversational coherence and diversity.
