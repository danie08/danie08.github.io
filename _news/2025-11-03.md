I am happy to share that I have started a new position as a **Researcher** in the [Language and Dialogue Technologies Group](https://land.fbk.eu/) at [Fondazione Bruno Kessler](https://www.fbk.eu/en/) in Trento.

This feels like a natural next step in the research trajectory I have been building over the last few years, from profile-based dialogue datasets to work on data quality and interlocutor-aware generation. I am excited to keep developing that line of work in an environment where conversational AI, language technologies, and evaluation can be studied together.
