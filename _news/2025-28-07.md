From July 27th to August 1st, I attended [ACL 2025](https://2025.aclweb.org/) in Vienna, where I presented our poster on the paper:  
**[When Harry Meets Superman: The Role of the Interlocutor in Persona-Based Dialogue Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.879/)**.

The poster session was an incredible experience! I had so many engaging conversations about how large language models adapt not only to their assigned persona, but also to the persona of their interlocutor. It was exciting to hear different insightful questions and feedback: they made the session memorable! 🚀
