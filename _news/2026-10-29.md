Our paper [Stranger, Fan, or Peer? A Systematic Study on the Role of Interlocutor in Persona-Based Dialogue Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.445.pdf), with Malvina Nissim and Marco Guerini, is published in the EMNLP 2026 proceedings. It examines how access to speaker biographies during training, generation and evaluation shapes adaptation, copying and information leakage.
